## LGA to Hoboken

The best-value transit route is the free Q70 to Jackson Heights, the F train to Herald Square, then PATH from 33rd Street to Hoboken. Allow roughly 75-105 minutes and check holiday-weekend alerts. A ride app is simpler with luggage but traffic-sensitive.
