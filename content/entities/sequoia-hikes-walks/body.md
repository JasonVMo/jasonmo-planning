## Foothills

- **Paradise Creek:** about 3.4 miles including the current road approach; start early in heat.
- **Marble Falls:** 7.8 miles round trip; exposed and much harder in high temperatures.

## Giant Forest and Lodgepole

- **General Sherman and Congress Trail:** the essential grove walk; Congress is about two miles.
- **Tokopah Falls:** 3.4 miles round trip and a useful easier fallback.
- **Lakes Trail / Heather Lake:** verify current route mileage, gain, and conditions before choosing it as the long day.

## Grant Grove and Cedar Grove

- **Big Baldy:** 4.4 miles with about 600 feet of gain.
- **Mist Falls:** about 8 miles; allow four to five hours after the long drive.
- **Zumwalt Meadow and Roaring River Falls:** shorter scenic options.

Use the [NPS day-hike pages](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/dayhikes.htm) and current trail report rather than email-derived mileage.
