## East side

- **Gorham Mountain Loop:** 3.5 miles, moderate and rocky.
- **Beehive Loop:** 1.4 miles with iron rungs and exposed cliff travel; only in dry conditions.
- **Great Head:** 1.7 miles over rocky coastal terrain.

## Central mountains

- **Jordan Pond Path:** 3.3 miles with boulders and narrow boardwalk on the west side.
- **Pemetic Loop:** 4 miles with scrambling, rungs, and a ladder.
- **Cadillac South Ridge:** 7.1 miles and a substantial exposed ridge day.

## West side and Schoodic

- **Beech Mountain:** 1.2 miles; drive because there is no direct trailhead transit.
- **Ship Harbor:** 1.3-mile easier coastal loop.
- **Schoodic:** choose among 7.5 miles of linked trails.

Routes 4, 5, and 6 do not stop at the Bubble Rock trailhead. Use Jordan Pond for a bus-based approach or drive with a parking backup.
