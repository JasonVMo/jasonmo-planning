## Shape of the trip

- **Base:** Three Rivers, outside the Ash Mountain entrance.
- **Giant Forest day:** General Sherman, Congress Trail, Moro Rock, and Crescent Meadow.
- **Long hike day:** choose a current-condition-appropriate route from Lodgepole or Wolverton.
- **Kings Canyon day:** Grant Grove plus either a scenic Cedar Grove day or one substantial hike.
- **Flex day:** hold for weather, smoke, construction, or Crystal Cave availability.

Check the [NPS current conditions](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/conditions.htm) before each long driving day.
