## Weather

The September 8 point forecast did not yet reach the trip dates. Recheck Three Rivers, Foothills, and Lodgepole separately: their elevation difference can produce hot foothills and cold high-country mornings on the same day.

## Book and recheck

- Reserve Crystal Cave promptly if desired.
- Check NPS alerts, smoke, road conditions, and construction each evening.
- Confirm weekend access to Moro Rock and Crescent Meadow after shuttle season.
- Keep one flex day for smoke, heat, or road delays.

## Pack

Carry substantial water, sun protection, a warm layer, rain/wind shell, paper/offline maps, and food storage appropriate for bear country.
