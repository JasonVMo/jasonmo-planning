# Acadia restaurants

Local and practical dining around Bar Harbor with seasonal-hour caveats.

<!-- BEGIN AGENT-MANAGED: research-summary -->

## Options

- Jordan Pond House for a timed park meal; reservations are useful.
- Atlantic Brewing in Town Hill for local beer and casual food.
- Thirsty Whale for a flexible downtown meal.
- Havana for a special dinner.
- A seasonal lobster pound in Trenton or on the quiet side for local character.

Late-season hours can change. Carry lunch on long hike and Schoodic days rather than depending on one concession.
