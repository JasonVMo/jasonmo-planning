# Acadia things to do

Coast, carriage roads, Cadillac, Schoodic, west-side sights, and adaptable day plans.

## Four-day framework

1. East-side coast: Sand Beach, Great Head or dry-weather Beehive, open Ocean Path sections, and Gorham Mountain.
2. Jordan Pond and central mountains: Jordan Pond Path, South Bubble from the pond, or dry-weather Pemetic.
3. West side and carriage roads: Beech Mountain alternatives, Southwest Harbor, Ship Harbor, and Bass Harbor Head Light.
4. Schoodic Peninsula: shoreline stops and short hikes; use October 7 to avoid the largest cruise day.

Cadillac Summit Road requires a separate vehicle reservation through October 25. Current alerts include southern Ocean Path, Schoodic Mountain Road, and eastern Beech Mountain Loop closures.
