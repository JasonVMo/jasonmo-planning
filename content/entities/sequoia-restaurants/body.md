# Sequoia restaurants

Flexible dining and supply options around Three Rivers and inside the parks.

<!-- BEGIN AGENT-MANAGED: research-summary -->

## Three Rivers

- Gateway Restaurant is a practical sit-down meal near the entrance.
- River View Grill is useful after a park day.
- Reimer's is a local candy and ice-cream stop.
- Sierra Subs reports a September 11-October 5 closure, so it is unavailable.

## Park days

Lodgepole, Wuksachi, Grant Grove, and Cedar Grove concessions are seasonal. Carry lunch and backup food for Kings Canyon rather than depending on late-season service. Fill the fuel tank in Three Rivers; no gasoline is sold inside park boundaries.
