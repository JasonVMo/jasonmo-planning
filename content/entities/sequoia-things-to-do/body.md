# Sequoia things to do

Giant sequoia groves, caves, viewpoints, scenic drives, and a practical Kings Canyon day.

<!-- BEGIN AGENT-MANAGED: research-summary -->

## High-priority stops

- General Sherman Tree and the two-mile Congress Trail.
- Moro Rock, Tunnel Log, Crescent Meadow, and Big Trees Trail.
- Crystal Cave only with an advance guided-tour ticket; tickets are not sold at the cave.
- Grant Grove and the General Grant Tree on the Kings Canyon day.

## Kings Canyon day

Leave Three Rivers early. Choose either a scenic day with Grant Grove, overlooks, Grizzly Falls, Roaring River Falls, and Zumwalt Meadow, or drive directly to Roads End for Mist Falls. Do not attempt every stop plus the full hike.

## Access notes

The 2026 summer shuttle ended before this trip. Mineral King construction creates long closure windows, so it is a backup rather than a core day. Recheck [roads](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/road-conditions.htm), [alerts](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/conditions.htm), and [Crystal Cave](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/crystal-cave.htm).
