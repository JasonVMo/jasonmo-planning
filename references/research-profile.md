# Owner research profile

**Classification:** Private owner grounding  
**Basis:** Confirmed preferences supplied by the repository owner on
2026-09-07.  
**Use:** Rank and explain recommendations; do not treat preferences as evidence
for external facts or expose this reference directly to browser code.

## General travel style

- Favor local character, walkability, flexibility, and good value.
- Include can't-miss museums and museums that are quirky, unique, or kitschy.
- Look for quick roadside attractions, unusual shops, local markets, night
  markets, festivals, and experiences specific to the destination.
- Prioritize walkable neighborhoods and opportunities to experience local
  culture by roaming.
- Identify strong viewpoints and photo locations.

## Hikes and walks

- Hikes under 10 miles and up to 4,000 feet of climbing are acceptable.
- Include highly regarded moderate hikes.
- Include worthwhile short/easy walks that can fit into a larger driving day.
- Group options by region or practical access area.
- Report distance, elevation gain, difficulty, access constraints, drive or
  transit time, and source.
- Include images only through the validated trip-banner
  provenance/publication contract. Continue linking to authoritative pages
  rather than copying images for every other content surface.

## Restaurants

- Prefer good mid-range value, local character, farm-to-table cooking, and
  distinctive or interesting food.
- Fine dining is worthwhile only for a genuinely can't-miss establishment.
- Avoid tourist traps unless a unique view or another special attribute
  justifies them.
- Include cuisines of all kinds and vary recommendations across a trip rather
  than repeating one category every day.

## Lodging

- Generally avoid large resorts and all-inclusive properties.
- Prefer good value or meaningful local character.
- Prioritize location and walkability.
- Consider homes or apartment rentals for multi-day stays when the price is
  reasonable.
- Compare total cost, cancellation terms, practical location, parking/transit,
  and check-in constraints rather than nightly rate alone.

## Flights

- Home airport is Seattle.
- Prefer Alaska Airlines when practical because the owner has status.
- Prefer Alaska partners, including American Airlines, when Alaska is not
  available.
- Prefer direct flights and avoid red-eyes when possible.
- Avoid basic economy.
- Present flights in a consistent structured tile with carrier/number, airport
  sequence, local departure/arrival times, connection duration, status, and
  trip relationship.
- Keep confirmation codes and loyalty identifiers out of summaries, search,
  calendar entries, and broader-audience projections.
