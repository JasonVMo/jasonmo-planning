## Cadillac Summit Road refresh (2026-10-02)

Observed from current NPS and Recreation.gov pages: vehicle reservations are required May 20–October 25, 2026; 30% of inventory opens 90 days ahead and 70% opens at 10 a.m. ET two days ahead. October 1–15 Sunrise entry is 5:30–7 a.m. and Daytime entry is 7:30 a.m.–6 p.m. Sunrise is a 90-minute entry window; Daytime is 30 minutes. The $6 ticket is per vehicle and is separate from the park entrance pass; reservation confirmation QR should be saved offline. The summit road closes to vehicles at 9 p.m., does not allow re-entry, and is not served by Island Explorer. Vehicles over 21 feet, RVs and trailers are prohibited.

Recommended: choose a day with the clearest summit forecast; use Daytime unless sunrise is the priority. NPS recommends going directly to the reservation area to avoid missing the timed entry because of Park Loop Road traffic. The October 5–10 two-day release dates in the page are calculated from the official two-day release rule.
