## Four-day framework

1. East-side coast: Sand Beach, Great Head or dry-weather Beehive, open Ocean Path sections, and Gorham Mountain.
2. Jordan Pond and central mountains: Jordan Pond Path, South Bubble from the pond, or dry-weather Pemetic.
3. West side and carriage roads: Beech Mountain alternatives, Southwest Harbor, Ship Harbor, and Bass Harbor Head Light.
4. Schoodic Peninsula: shoreline stops and short hikes; use October 7 to avoid the largest cruise day.

Recheck NPS alerts for trail, road, and facility conditions before each outing.

## Cadillac Mountain Summit Road drive

Cadillac Summit Road is a three-mile drive from Park Loop Road to the 1,530-foot summit. A timed vehicle reservation is required from May 20 through October 25, 2026. This is separate from the Acadia entrance pass. The road has two-way traffic, signed pullouts, and two summit parking areas; RVs and trailers are prohibited, as are vehicles longer than 21 feet including racks and hitch attachments. The Island Explorer does not go to the summit.

**Best fit for October 5–10:** choose the day with the clearest summit forecast and reserve a Daytime entry. A morning slot avoids the busiest middle of the day without requiring a pre-dawn start. If watching sunrise is the main goal, Sunrise is the right choice, but plan for darkness, cold, wind, and the chance that fog or clouds hide the view. The NPS notes lighter park traffic before 8 a.m. and after 5 p.m.; Cadillac itself is popular at sunrise and sunset.

For October 1–15, Sunrise reservations allow entry from **5:30–7:00 a.m.**; Daytime entry is **7:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m.** Sunrise is a 90-minute arrival window and Daytime is 30 minutes. You may stay until the summit road closes to vehicles at 9 p.m., but the reservation does not allow re-entry or assign a parking space.

### How to reserve

Book in advance on [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/timed-entry/400000); tickets are not sold at the park. The fee is **$6 per vehicle**, in addition to the park entrance pass. Thirty percent of Sunrise and Daytime tickets are released 90 days ahead; the remaining 70 percent go on sale at **10 a.m. Eastern Time, two days before** the visit. Sign in before the release and have the date and ticket type ready. The release dates for this stay are:

| -------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Monday, October 5    | Saturday, October 3 at 10 a.m. ET  |
| Tuesday, October 6   | Sunday, October 4 at 10 a.m. ET    |
| Wednesday, October 7 | Monday, October 5 at 10 a.m. ET    |
| Thursday, October 8  | Tuesday, October 6 at 10 a.m. ET   |
| Friday, October 9    | Wednesday, October 7 at 10 a.m. ET |
| Saturday, October 10 | Thursday, October 8 at 10 a.m. ET  |

Save the confirmation QR code offline because cell service can be unreliable. Sunrise is limited to one reservation per vehicle in seven days; Daytime is limited to one per day. Check [NPS reservation details](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/vehicle_reservations.htm) before booking for any rule updates.

### Driving approach and backup

From Bar Harbor, take Route 3 to Park Loop Road and follow signs for Cadillac Summit Road. The summit spur can be reached near either end of the loop. With a reservation, the NPS recommends driving directly to Cadillac rather than stopping elsewhere first, since traffic can make you miss the entry window. Review the [Park Loop Road guide](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/park-loop-road.htm) and [current park alerts](https://www.nps.gov/acad/park-alerts-acad.json) before leaving.

If tickets are unavailable, the Island Explorer is not a summit alternative. The NPS says visitors arriving by foot, bicycle, or taxi do not need a vehicle reservation; the Cadillac South Ridge Trail is a strenuous hike option. Check current trail conditions and daylight before choosing it. For an easier drive-free view, use Ocean Drive or another open coastal overlook.

## Walks, curiosities, and indoor stops

- **Bar Island land bridge:** walk from downtown Bar Harbor at low tide; the gravel bar is exposed for only 1.5 hours either side of low tide. Continue the short wooded trail for a view over town. Check the tide table first and leave as the tide rises; walking across is free, though a park entrance pass is required in season.
- **Bar Harbor Museum in the Streets:** self-guided historic walk among 26 downtown panels, with two longer village-perimeter routes. Panels are outdoors, so visit any time in daylight; the downtown circuit is easy to fit between meals or shops.
- **Abbe Museum (downtown):** Wabanaki-led museum focused on Wabanaki history and living culture. Listed hours: Monday–Saturday, 10 a.m.–5 p.m.; adult admission $18. The Sieur de Monts location is closed for maintenance.
- **Dorr Museum of Natural History:** student-designed natural-history exhibits at College of the Atlantic; fall hours listed as weekdays 10 a.m.–5 p.m. and Saturday 1–4 p.m.; admission by donation. Walkable from downtown on the Route 3 multi-use path; Island Explorer Route 2 stops at the campus.
- **Oceanarium & Education Center:** lobster and Maine-coast exhibits plus marsh boardwalk; fall hours are Wednesday–Sunday, 9 a.m.–4 p.m., with admission charged. Island Explorer serves it during the season; confirm daily programming and the current schedule.
- **Asticou Azalea Garden:** Japanese-inspired garden and pond in Northeast Harbor; open daily dawn to dusk through November 1, with a suggested $5 donation. Pair it with the nearby village and harbor walk.
- **Thuya Garden:** woodland garden and historic lodge in Northeast Harbor; open daily dawn to dusk through October 13, with lodge hours 10 a.m.–5 p.m. and a suggested $5 donation. Parking is limited.
- **Somesville:** pause at the small harbor, historic bridge, and village setting on the way to the west side; a useful brief photo stop when driving Route 102.
- **Bass Harbor Head Light:** dramatic working lighthouse and rocky shoreline viewpoint; the grounds are a short walk from the parking area. Parking is very limited, and visitors cannot enter the tower; arrive early and follow posted safety signs.

These businesses and gardens operate seasonally. Confirm hours before driving, especially for October 12–14, when many attractions change to reduced schedules or close.
