The parks are open 24 hours daily and do not require an entrance reservation. "Park access" below describes an unstaffed trail or roadside area, not staffed services or a recommendation to visit outside safe daylight hours.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Foothills Visitor Center](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm) | Daily 8 am-5 pm                    | Short orientation, conditions, maps, restrooms, and water near the Ash Mountain entrance.                                                             |

## Closed or constrained

- **Crystal Cave:** tours have ended for the entire 2026 season. Remove it from the active itinerary.
- **Shuttles:** 2026 summer service ended September 7, and Kings Canyon has no in-park shuttle.
- **Mineral King:** major daytime construction closures make it a poor spontaneous backup.
- **Parking:** General Sherman and Giant Forest Museum lots can fill early, especially on weekends.

Recheck [roads](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/road-conditions.htm), [current conditions](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/conditions.htm), and live alerts each morning.
