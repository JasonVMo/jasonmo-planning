## What the drive involves

Cadillac Summit Road is a three-mile paved spur from Park Loop Road to the 1,530-foot summit. The road is narrow and two-way; drive slowly, watch for walkers and cyclists, and use only signed pullouts and summit parking. Two parking areas and paved summit walks are available. Island Explorer does not go to the summit.

## When to go

**Recommended for this trip:** choose the clearest forecast day, then reserve a daytime entry. A morning slot avoids the busiest middle of the day; a later slot can also work if the summit forecast is clear. NPS says park traffic is generally lighter before 8 a.m. and after 5 p.m., though Cadillac itself draws sunrise and sunset crowds. If your priority is sunrise, reserve a Sunrise entry and plan for darkness, cold, wind, and limited visibility risk.

For October 1–15, 2026, Sunrise reservations allow entry from **5:30–7:00 a.m.** and Daytime reservations from **7:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m.** These are arrival windows, not a fixed length of stay. A reservation allows you to stay until the road closes to vehicles at 9 p.m. and does not allow re-entry. Check the date-specific forecast and [NPS alerts](https://www.nps.gov/acad/park-alerts-acad.json) before committing to the drive; fog or clouds can obscure the summit.

## Booking a vehicle reservation

A vehicle reservation is required to drive Cadillac Summit Road May 20–October 25, 2026. Book **online in advance through [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/timed-entry/400000)**; reservations are not sold at the park. The fee is **$6 per vehicle**, separate from the Acadia entrance pass. One ticket covers the vehicle, not each passenger. Bring the park pass as well, and save the reservation QR code offline because cell service can be unreliable.

The inventory releases at **10 a.m. Eastern Time**:

- 30% of Sunrise and Daytime tickets are released 90 days before the visit.
- The remaining 70% are released two days before the visit. Sign in before 10 a.m., choose the ticket type and date, and be ready to complete checkout; popular times can sell out.

For the Acadia stay, the two-day releases are:

| Visit date           | Remaining inventory released       |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Monday, October 5    | Saturday, October 3 at 10 a.m. ET  |
| Tuesday, October 6   | Sunday, October 4 at 10 a.m. ET    |
| Wednesday, October 7 | Monday, October 5 at 10 a.m. ET    |
| Thursday, October 8  | Tuesday, October 6 at 10 a.m. ET   |
| Friday, October 9    | Wednesday, October 7 at 10 a.m. ET |
| Saturday, October 10 | Thursday, October 8 at 10 a.m. ET  |

Sunrise tickets are limited to one per vehicle in any seven-day period; Daytime tickets are limited to one per vehicle per day. The Sunrise entry window is 90 minutes; Daytime is 30 minutes. Reservations do not assign a parking space, and there is no re-entry. Vehicles longer than 21 feet including racks or hitch attachments, RVs, and trailers are prohibited.

## Getting there and a backup

From Bar Harbor, follow Route 3 to Park Loop Road and the signed Cadillac Summit Road turnoff. The summit road can be reached near either end of the Park Loop Road. If you have a reservation, NPS recommends driving directly to Cadillac rather than making other stops first, since traffic can make you miss your entry window. Check the [Park Loop Road guide](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/park-loop-road.htm) for current direction and closure information.

If tickets are gone, the Island Explorer is not a substitute for the summit drive. The NPS says visitors arriving on foot, bicycle, or taxi do not need a vehicle reservation; for a hike, consider the Cadillac South Ridge Trail, and check current trail conditions and daylight before choosing it. For a drive-free coastal view, use Ocean Drive or another open overlook instead.
