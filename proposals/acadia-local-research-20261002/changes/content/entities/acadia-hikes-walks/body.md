Distances and elevation gains are approximate route-planning figures; footing, weather, and chosen variants change effort. Acadia trails are rocky, and wet granite can be slippery. Check current NPS alerts before setting out.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ocean Path:** up to 4.4 mi round trip; mostly level                                       | Easy coastal walk between Sand Beach and Otter Point, with shorter turnarounds at Thunder Hole or Otter Cliff.                                                                                                                           | **Island Explorer Route 4** stops along the corridor, including Sand Beach, Thunder Hole, and Otter Cliff; or drive to Park Loop Road lots.                                                         |
| **Great Head:** about 1.5 mi loop; modest gain (~300 ft)                                    | Rocky headland loop above Sand Beach; uneven footing and exposed ocean edges.                                                                                                                                                            | **Route 4** to Sand Beach, then walk to the east end of the beach; or drive to Sand Beach and walk across.                                                                                          |
| **Beehive:** about 1.4 mi loop; ~500 ft                                                     | Short, strenuous iron-rung route with exposed cliff sections. Choose only in dry conditions and if comfortable with heights; use the Bowl bypass to avoid the rungs.                                                                     | **Route 4** to Sand Beach, then walk to the trailhead; or drive to the Sand Beach lot.                                                                                                              |
| **Gorham Mountain:** 1.5 mi one way on the NPS-listed trail (longer loop options); ~500 ft  | Moderate rocky climb to open views. Can be paired with Ocean Path; expect rough granite.                                                                                                                                                 | **Route 4** serves the Gorham Mountain parking loop; or drive to the Park Loop Road trailhead.                                                                                                      |

**Shuttle planning:** the 2026 Island Explorer season runs through October 12, covering the Acadia stay. Route 4 serves the Park Loop Road; Route 5 serves Jordan Pond every 30 minutes in the posted fall schedule. Verify the live timetable and stop details before leaving. The bus does not serve Cadillac summit. For itineraries that combine distant stops, leave room for waits and use the myStop app for live tracking.
