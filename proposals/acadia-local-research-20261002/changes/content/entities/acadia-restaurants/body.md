## In and around the park

- **Jordan Pond House:** classic popovers, tea, and a full menu beside Jordan Pond. Restaurant service is listed daily from 11 a.m.; the carryout window is listed 9 a.m.–4 p.m. Many tables are walk-in, but check current service status before planning a meal around a hike.
- **Atlantic Brewing, Town Hill:** brewery and barbecue at 15 Knox Road; listed daily 11 a.m.–7 p.m. Brewery tours are listed Friday at 3 p.m. and Saturday/Sunday at noon and 3 p.m.
- **Atlantic Brewing Midtown:** casual brewery food and beer at 52 Cottage Street in Bar Harbor; listed daily 11 a.m.–9 p.m., convenient after a day in town.
- **Thrive Juice Bar & Kitchen:** quick breakfast, juice, and lunch at 6 Rodick Place; fall hours listed daily 7:30 a.m.–2 p.m., with the 2026 season ending October 18.
- **The Reading Room at Bar Harbor Inn:** waterfront fine dining for a special dinner; 2026 season listed May 1–November 9, breakfast 7–10:30 a.m. and dinner 5–9 p.m.

## Seasonal planning

The Island Explorer serves downtown Bar Harbor and Jordan Pond through October 12, which covers the main Acadia stay. On October 13–14 the shuttle is no longer in its 2026 operating season, so plan those days around driving or walkable Bar Harbor options. Hours and last service dates can change; check the restaurant’s current page or call before making a special trip. Carry food and water on longer hikes and Schoodic days rather than relying on a concession being open.
