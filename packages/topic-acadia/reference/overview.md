## Best current trip shape

The strongest starting plan is:

1. **Friday, October 9:** fly Seattle to Bangor, pick up a round-trip Bangor rental car, and continue to Mount Desert Island.
2. **October 10–14:** spend about five days based in downtown Bar Harbor. Front-load crowded east-side sights and shuttle-dependent hikes before Island Explorer service ends on October 12.
3. **Thursday, October 15:** return the car at Bangor and take a nonstop flight to whichever NYC airport is most convenient to the son's school.
4. **October 15–18:** visit New York.
5. **Sunday, October 18:** fly nonstop from JFK or Newark to Seattle.

This is a planning recommendation, not a booked itinerary. The travel report also identifies a cheaper sampled Portland arrival, but its longer drive and loss of vacation time make Bangor the better default.

## Decisions to make next

- **Confirm the son's school/location in New York.** That determines whether LGA, JFK, or EWR is the sensible NYC airport.
- **Choose the Acadia/NYC split.** The research assumes roughly October 9–15 in transit/Acadia and October 15–18 in New York. Compare an October 14 Bangor departure if airfare or an extra NYC day matters more than a fifth Acadia day.
- **Place a refundable Bar Harbor lodging hold.** Start with Acadia Hotel–Downtown, The Inn on Mount Desert, Yellow House Inn, or Atlantic Oceanside; compare one Southwest Harbor alternative if quieter evenings are a priority.
- **Price the complete open-jaw itinerary together.** Compare SEA→BGR / BGR→NYC / NYC→SEA, including bags, seats, rental-car total, and cancellation terms—not just headline fares.
- **Choose hiking appetite.** Jordan Pond + South Bubble, Gorham Mountain, and Cadillac South Ridge form the best balanced three-hike set. Add Beehive only for hikers comfortable with heights and only in dry, calm conditions.

## Dates and constraints that affect multiple plans

- **October 9–12:** peak-foliage holiday weekend conditions may mean heavy traffic, scarce rooms, and crowded trailheads.
- **October 12:** the Island Explorer's reduced fall service ends. Use it before then for east-side trailheads where parking is difficult.
- **Mid-October:** foliage is usually near peak, but exact color timing cannot be guaranteed. Follow Maine's weekly foliage reports starting in September.
- **October 15 onward:** several seasonal services begin closing or reducing hours, though Park Loop Road, Jordan Pond House, and major visitor infrastructure are scheduled to remain available during this window.
- **Short daylight and variable weather:** sunset moves from about 5:59 p.m. on October 9 to 5:43 p.m. on October 18. Wet leaves and granite can materially change hike difficulty.

## Suggested Acadia priorities

For a first visit, protect time for:

- Park Loop Road combined with Ocean Path, Sand Beach, Thunder Hole, and Otter Cliffs;
- Jordan Pond and South Bubble, ideally early in the day;
- one Cadillac Mountain experience—South Ridge hike in good weather, or a reserved summit-road visit;
- a carriage-road walk or bike ride;
- the Quiet Side around Somes Sound, Southwest Harbor, Seawall, and Bass Harbor Head Light;
- Schoodic Peninsula as the best full-day add-on if weather and time permit.
