# Travel arrangements: Seattle → Acadia → New York → Seattle

**Trip window:** October 9–18, 2026  
**Research completed:** August 23, 2026 (Pacific Time); fare matrix refreshed August 23, 2026  
**Assumptions:** two adults in economy; the whole trip fits inside the stated window; Acadia is the first stop and New York City the second; the New York school/location is not yet known; fares below are per person unless stated otherwise; checked-bag, seat, rental-car, parking, and toll costs are extra unless explicitly included. Alaska status makes an Alaska-operated flight preferable when schedule and total price are reasonably competitive.

## Bottom line

The best default itinerary remains:

1. **Friday, October 9: SEA → BGR**, one stop; pick up a rental car at Bangor.
2. Spend roughly **October 10–14 in Acadia/Bar Harbor**.
3. **Thursday, October 15: return the car at BGR and fly nonstop BGR → the NYC airport most convenient to the son's school.**
4. Spend October 15–18 in New York.
5. **Sunday, October 18: fly EWR → SEA nonstop on Alaska if competitively priced; check Alaska from JFK second, then United from EWR.**

Why: Bangor is only 50 miles/about one hour from Acadia, has major-airline rental cars, and has daily nonstop service to the principal New York airports. It avoids a 490–500-mile drive to New York and the potentially expensive one-way rental/drop logistics. The best fully surfaced inbound remains **American SEA–PHL–BGR on Friday, October 9 for $295**, taking 8h06. The strongest price-led alternative is now **American SEA–ORD–PWM on Saturday, October 10 for $188**, taking 9h12, but Portland adds a 2.5–3-hour drive to Acadia.

The earlier $123 Portland result could not be reproduced in the refreshed indexed results and should be treated as expired. The current surfaced October 10 option is $188. All prices below are research snapshots, not quotes or guarantees.

### Rental-car implications

- **Reserve at the same time as airfare.** October foliage demand can constrain inventory, especially at BHB.
- For the recommended plan, book a **round-trip BGR rental** and return it immediately before the New York flight. This makes pricing transparent and avoids keeping a car in NYC.
- If landing at PWM, compare two structures: return at BGR before flying to NYC, or return at PWM and fly PWM–NYC. Both are one-way Maine rentals, so price the exact airport pair rather than assuming no fee.
- A Maine-to-New-York one-way rental must be explicitly booked that way. Drop charges vary by company, station, date, and vehicle; never assume “unlimited miles” means “no relocation fee.” Also add fuel, tolls, and NYC parking.
- NPS notes rental cars are available at BGR and BHB. A car remains the most flexible choice for trailheads in October; the free Island Explorer operates only through mid-October, so exact 2026 end dates matter. [NPS brochure](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/park-brochure.htm)

## Acadia to New York: fly or drive?

### Recommendation: fly nonstop from Bangor

Bangor Airport's official airline page lists Delta nonstop to JFK/LGA, United to EWR, and American seasonally to LGA. Current schedules show daily service; Google Flights reports the fastest trip at about 1h31 and nonstops every day. [BGR airlines](https://flybangor.com/passenger-information/airlines/) · [Google Flights BGR–NYC](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-bangor-to-new-york.html?gl=US&hl=en-US)

Allow roughly an hour from Bar Harbor to BGR, rental-car return time, and normal airport buffer. Even door-to-door, flying should take around 4–6 hours versus an all-day drive. Choose the NYC airport based on the son's school:

- **LGA:** generally best for Manhattan's east side, Queens, and many Brooklyn trips.
- **JFK:** useful for Brooklyn/Queens and Delta schedules; less convenient for much of Manhattan.
- **EWR:** often best for New Jersey and Manhattan's west side; United has nonstop BGR service.

Do not optimize the fare until the school location is known: a $30–50 saving can disappear in airport transfers and time.

### Driving case

Bar Harbor to New York is roughly **490–500 miles and 8.5–9 hours without meaningful stops**; budget 10–12 hours with food, traffic, and the Boston/Connecticut corridor. One current route estimate is 506 miles/8h29. [Route Outlook](https://routeoutlook.com/popular-routes-near-me/bar-harbor-to-new-york)

Driving is worthwhile only if the transit itself is a desired experience—e.g. an overnight in Portland, Portsmouth, Boston, or coastal Connecticut—or if a group-sized airfare saving exceeds the one-way rental premium, fuel, tolls, parking, and the value of a lost day. For two adults on the sampled $234 BGR–LGA flight, airfare is about **$468 total** before bags. Driving may look cheaper on fuel alone, but the one-way drop charge and NYC parking are the swing items. Get a live rental quote with the exact NYC return station before choosing it.

**Conclusion:** for a nine-night trip split between two destinations, drive back to Bangor and fly. Do not drive all the way to NYC unless the family explicitly wants a road-trip day or overnight stop.

## New York to Seattle

There are daily nonstops to SEA from:

- **JFK:** Alaska, Delta, and JetBlue;
- **EWR:** Alaska and United;
- **LGA:** no nonstop to SEA—expect a connection.

Google Flights puts the fastest nonstop around **6h01**. [NYC–SEA route overview](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-new-york-to-seattle.html?gl=US&hl=en-US) For Sunday Oct 18, search **EWR first and JFK second**, with the nonstop filter locked on. Favor Alaska because of existing status when its schedule and total fare are competitive. The $185 two-stop result is not eligible under the direct-flight requirement; its 19h43 itinerary consumes almost an extra day and has more disruption risk.

Practical carrier/airport pairing:

- staying in New Jersey or near Penn Station: price **United/Alaska from EWR**;
- staying in Brooklyn/Queens or convenient to the LIRR: price **Alaska/Delta/JetBlue from JFK**;
- if Alaska loyalty or a companion fare matters, search both EWR and JFK (but verify fare eligibility on an open-jaw itinerary).

## Booking checklist

1. Confirm the son's school address, then rank LGA/JFK/EWR by ground convenience.
2. Price all four inbound cells: **SEA–BGR and SEA–PWM on both Oct 9 and Oct 10**. Attach the full matching-airport rental quote and drive time to each.
3. For each viable arrival airport, price the matching **BGR–NYC or PWM–NYC nonstop on both Oct 14 and Oct 15**.
4. Price **Alaska EWR–SEA on Oct 18 nonstop first**, then Alaska JFK–SEA, then United EWR–SEA; compare total cost after status benefits, bags, and seats.
5. Compare one multi-city reservation with three separate one-ways.
6. Place refundable lodging/rental holds before airfare if possible, then turn on Google Flights price tracking.
7. Recheck Cape Air only if BHB's 10-mile proximity is especially appealing; leave a generous BOS connection buffer and verify baggage rules for the small aircraft.
