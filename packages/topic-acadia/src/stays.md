# Where to stay near Acadia

**Likely Acadia nights:** October 5–9 or 10, 2026  
**Research checked:** August 24, 2026

## Recommendation

Stay in **downtown Bar Harbor for the entire Acadia portion**. For a first visit of only four or five days, being able to walk to dinner and reach the east-side highlights quickly is worth more than the quieter atmosphere or modest savings elsewhere. It also avoids spending vacation time changing hotels. Look first for a refundable room with off-street parking and breakfast.

**Best-fit shortlist:** [Yellow House Inn](https://www.yellowhousemaine.com/) for a small, characterful inn near the waterfront (its site explicitly lists a 2026 season through October 23); [Acadia Hotel–Downtown](https://www.acadiahotel.com/) for the most central location by the Village Green; [The Inn on Mount Desert](https://www.theinnonmountdesert.com/) for breakfast and parking; or [Atlantic Oceanside](https://www.barharbormainehotel.com/) for ocean views, easier parking, and more hotel amenities about a mile from the center. Compare the total refundable rate and room type, not just the headline price.

Choose **Southwest Harbor** instead only if quiet evenings and the western side of Mount Desert Island are the main objective. **Northeast Harbor** is the best geographically central alternative but has a thin evening dining scene. **Ellsworth or Trenton** is a sensible value fallback, not the preferred vacation experience.

## Comparing the bases

Drive times below are realistic planning ranges in ordinary conditions, not promises. Park Loop Road is partly one-way, and October foliage traffic, road work, weather, and time spent finding a trailhead space can all add time. The [NPS parking page](https://www.nps.gov/acad/parking.htm) is useful on the day of a visit.

### Why not split the stay?

Four or five nights is too short to gain much from moving between Bar Harbor and the Quiet Side. Southwest Harbor's west-side sights make an easy full day from Bar Harbor. A split becomes more attractive at six or more nights, especially for travelers planning several western-island hikes.

## Lodging ideas by area

This is a fit shortlist, not an availability claim. Rates and inventory change continuously; book direct where the cancellation terms are competitive and verify parking, breakfast hours, deposits, and the exact final operating night.

### Bar Harbor

- **[Yellow House Inn](https://www.yellowhousemaine.com/):** intimate B&B/boutique feel, a short walk from the waterfront and restaurants. The strongest seasonal evidence of the group: the inn explicitly says it is open May 15–October 23, 2026.
- **[Acadia Hotel–Downtown](https://www.acadiahotel.com/):** right beside the Village Green, ideal for leaving the car parked at dinner and potentially using the shuttle. Confirm which building and parking arrangement apply to the selected room.
- **[The Inn on Mount Desert](https://www.theinnonmountdesert.com/):** central but slightly removed from the busiest waterfront blocks; included breakfast and off-street parking are valuable for early hiking days.
- **[Atlantic Oceanside](https://www.barharbormainehotel.com/):** a larger oceanfront property with breakfast, indoor pool, and easy parking. It is not as convenient for walking to dinner; check the precise building and view attached to the room category.
- **[Bar Harbor Condos](https://barharborcondos.com/):** useful for a kitchen, more space, and an in-town parking spot. Compare the all-in cost after cleaning and booking fees.

The Bar Harbor Chamber maintains both a broad [lodging directory](https://www.visitbarharbor.com/stay) and an [open-year-round directory](https://www.visitbarharbor.com/list/category/open-year-round-147/1000). “Year-round” is good evidence of operating resilience, but not proof of availability or unchanged daily service.

### Southwest Harbor and the Quiet Side

- **[Harbour Cottage Inn](https://harbourcottageinn.com/):** small historic inn in a quiet neighborhood, walkable to the village; a good match for a romantic, low-key stay. Confirm breakfast and bar dates for October.
- **[Cafe Drydock & Inn](https://www.cafedrydockinn.com/):** simple rooms in the village with an on-site casual restaurant; the official site says the inn is open year-round. Confirm the restaurant's fall schedule separately.
- **[The Claremont](https://www.theclaremonthotel.com/):** the splurge/destination-resort option with waterfront grounds. Ask which dining and resort amenities remain operating during the exact stay; an open room does not guarantee full summer service.

### Northeast Harbor

- **[The Asticou](https://www.opalcollection.com/asticou):** newly renovated, harbor-view resort lodging with on-site dining. It is the area's distinctive splurge, but confirm the 2026 closing date and October restaurant schedule before relying on either.
- **[Colonels Suites](https://www.colonelssuites.com/):** a practical in-village alternative above/near a casual local restaurant and bakery. Verify stair access, parking, and October dates directly.

### Ellsworth / Trenton

Favor a predictable chain or locally operated motel with breakfast and free parking rather than paying resort rates for a commuter base. This is a good backup if island rooms are unusually expensive, and it works especially well for the arrival night after a delayed flight. It is less appealing for every night because each park day begins and ends with Route 3.

## Restaurants worth seeking out

### Bar Harbor: the strongest overall dining base

- **[Havana](https://www.havanamaine.com/):** the best reservation-worthy dinner: Maine seafood and produce interpreted through Latin American flavors, plus a serious wine list. Reserve when the itinerary is firm and reconfirm the week of travel.
- **[Side Street Cafe](https://www.sidestreetbarharbor.com/):** family-run, creative comfort food—lobster rolls, sandwiches, tacos, salads, and customizable mac and cheese—well suited to a flexible post-hike meal. Parties of seven or fewer use its [online waitlist](https://www.sidestreetbarharbor.com/waitlist); the restaurant recommends joining about an hour ahead.
- **[The Thirsty Whale](https://www.thirstywhaletavern.com/):** unfussy local tavern for chowder, lobster roll, fish sandwiches, and Maine beer. Its official site currently lists service seven days a week, but October kitchen hours should still be checked that day.
- **[Jordan Pond House](https://jordanpondhouse.com/):** go for popovers, tradition, and the setting, ideally attached to a Jordan Pond or carriage-road day. It is the park's only restaurant; the [NPS 2026 operating calendar](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/hours.htm) lists restaurant service through October 26. Reserve and allow extra time for park traffic and parking.
- **[Mount Desert Island Ice Cream](https://www.mdiic.com/):** inventive small-batch flavors and a worthwhile local treat rather than generic dessert. Confirm which Bar Harbor location and fall hours are operating.

### Southwest Harbor

- **[Red Sky](https://www.redskyrestaurant.com/):** carefully prepared seasonal dinner in an intimate setting; the official site currently lists Wednesday–Sunday seating and takes reservations through Resy. This is the Quiet Side meal to plan around, but verify October dates.
- **[Cafe Drydock](https://www.cafedrydockinn.com/):** classic, friendly village choice for seafood and hearty fare; particularly convenient if staying upstairs. The inn is advertised year-round, but confirm restaurant days and hours.
- **[Beal's Lobster Pier](https://www.bealslobster.com/):** the quintessential working-waterfront lobster-pier experience. It is highly seasonal, so treat it as a bonus unless the restaurant directly confirms it will be open on the chosen evening.

### Northeast Harbor

- **[Colonel's Restaurant and Bakery](https://www.colonelsrestaurant.com/restaurant):** casual, family-owned local standby with baked goods and a broad menu—the most useful non-resort fallback in the village. Call for October hours.
- **[Dahlia's at The Asticou](https://www.opalcollection.com/asticou/dining/):** local seafood and produce with harbor views; a polished meal without driving to Bar Harbor. Confirm both a reservation and the hotel's seasonal service dates.

### Ellsworth

- **[Provender Kitchen + Bar](https://www.eatprovender.com/):** fresh, chef-driven food in downtown Ellsworth and the strongest destination dinner if staying on the mainland. Its current site offers reservations, but posted schedules conflict across pages, making a same-week check especially important.
- **[Fogtown Brewing](https://www.fogtownbrewing.com/):** locally brewed beer, casual food, and community programming; a distinctive relaxed alternative to a chain meal.
- **[Union River Lobster Pot](https://lobsterpot.com/):** traditional Maine lobster, steamers, chowder, and blueberry pie beside the river. The official site describes full service from mid-June through October, but does not promise every October date.

## October realities

- The proposed October 5–9/10 stay comes just before the Indigenous Peoples Day weekend (Monday, October 12), when foliage demand may rise. Reserve lodging early and favor refundable terms.
- The park itself is fully viable: NPS lists Park Loop Road open through December 1, Hulls Cove Visitor Center through October 31, and Jordan Pond House restaurant through October 26 on its [2026 operating-hours page](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/hours.htm).
- Restaurant hours are the weak link. A business may be “open for the season” but close two or three weekdays, end dinner early, or shut after a staffing change. The Chamber's [What's Open](https://www.visitbarharbor.com/open) page warns that information can change without notice. Check official sites 7–10 days out and call same-day for must-do meals.
- Do not choose a base on summer shuttle assumptions. Confirm the [Island Explorer schedule](https://www.exploreacadia.com/) for the exact 2026 dates; a rental car remains essential for this itinerary.
- Camping is possible but not a straightforward substitute for a hotel. NPS lists Blackwoods through October 19 at 10 a.m., while Seawall closes October 12 at 10 a.m.; reservations are required. See the [official campground page](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/camping.htm). Expect cold, rain, wind, and short daylight.

## Booking checklist

1. Hold a refundable downtown Bar Harbor room for October 5–9 or 10.
2. Compare the direct-booking total, including parking, taxes, breakfast, deposits, and minimum-stay rules.
3. Reserve one special dinner—Havana is the first choice—and Jordan Pond House once the hiking outline is known.
4. Keep the other dinners flexible for weather-driven itinerary changes; Side Street and Thirsty Whale are useful candidates.
5. Ten days before departure, recheck every hotel's amenities, every restaurant's October days/hours, the Island Explorer schedule, and NPS alerts.
