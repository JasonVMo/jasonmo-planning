# Travel plan

**Dates researched:** August 24, 2026 (Pacific time)  
**Trip:** Seattle → Bangor/Acadia → Hoboken/New York → Seattle, October 4–13, 2026  
**Fare assumptions:** economy, per traveler, before optional bags/seats. Airfares are volatile snapshots: click through and reprice the complete trip before purchasing. Where an exact-date fare was not exposed publicly, the table says so instead of presenting a route average as a quote.

## Recommended shape

1. Fly **SEA → BGR Sunday night, October 4**, arriving Monday, October 5. Pick up a rental car at BGR and drive to Mount Desert Island.
2. Choose either **Friday, October 9** (four Acadia days) or **Saturday, October 10** (five Acadia days) for BGR → NYC. Return the car at BGR and fly rather than driving to New York.
3. Fly **EWR → SEA nonstop Tuesday morning, October 13**, preferably Alaska.

This uses Bangor at both ends of the Maine rental, preserves the EWR preference for Hoboken, and avoids an exhausting 490–500-mile drive plus a one-way drop charge and New York parking.

## Seattle to Bangor — Sunday night, October 4

There is no SEA–BGR nonstop. Bangor's published arrivals make the useful connection gateways clear: American/Alaska codeshare service arrives from **PHL and ORD**, while United and Delta connect through their eastern hubs. The [official Bangor airline page](https://flybangor.com/passenger-information/airlines/) confirms American, Delta and United service; the [Port of Seattle nonstop map](https://www.portseattle.org/page/nonstop-domestic-routes) confirms that BGR itself is not a SEA nonstop.

The constraint is unusually important: only itineraries leaving after the Seahawks game are useful. Search with a **9:00 p.m. or later departure** filter and reject any itinerary that leaves Seattle on Sunday afternoon even if it is labeled an “overnight” trip.

## Bangor rental and drive to Acadia

The National Park Service places BGR about **50 miles from Acadia** (roughly one hour in ordinary conditions), versus 161 miles from Portland. [NPS directions](https://www.nps.gov/acad/planyourvisit/directions.htm) Pick up and return at BGR; book the car as soon as the flights are fixed because foliage-season inventory can tighten.

- Allow about **90 minutes** from touchdown to leaving BGR for baggage and rental pickup, then another **1–1¼ hours** to Bar Harbor without stops.
- Confirm the rental counter remains open for the actual flight arrival, and attach the flight number to the reservation.
- Keep the car through the Acadia stay. It is especially valuable in October when shuttle hours/service are reduced.
- Return it at BGR before the New York flight. A round-trip BGR rental is normally simpler than returning in New York or repositioning to PWM.
- If comparing a one-way drive to NYC, price the exact drop station and include the relocation fee, fuel, tolls and parking—not just the base daily rate.

## Booking checklist

- Search first as one **multi-city itinerary**: SEA–BGR / BGR–EWR / EWR–SEA. Then compare three one-ways. A protected itinerary and easier disruption handling can justify a modest premium.
- Verify **operating carrier**, not just the Alaska flight number, on American codeshares.
- Lock the Sunday departure filter to the actual earliest time you can reach SEA after the game.
- Avoid basic/Saver fares until checking elite-seat, change and cancellation restrictions.
- Reserve the BGR rental with free cancellation, then recheck its rate periodically.
- Reconfirm all schedules after booking: October regional frequencies are more vulnerable to seasonal timetable changes than the transcontinental flights.
