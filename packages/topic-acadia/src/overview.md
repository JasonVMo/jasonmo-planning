# Acadia + New York: October 4–13, 2026

## Recommended trip shape

Fly from Seattle to Bangor after the Seahawks game on **Sunday, October 4**, pick up a round-trip
Bangor rental car, and stay in **downtown Bar Harbor**. Spend four or five weather-flexible days in
Acadia, return the car at Bangor, and take the late-morning United nonstop to Newark. After the family
visit in Hoboken, fly Alaska nonstop from Newark to Seattle on **Tuesday, October 13**.

The main unresolved choice is the Maine-to-New York date:

- **Friday, October 9:** four Acadia days and three-plus days around Hoboken/NYC. This is the balanced
  recommendation.
- **Saturday, October 10:** five Acadia days, but only two full New York days. Choose this if hiking
  and weather flexibility matter more than city time.

Bangor is about 50 miles from Acadia, and using it for both ends of the rental avoids a one-way fee.
EWR is markedly more convenient than LGA for Stevens Institute. Exact-date flight choices and the
August 24 fare snapshot are on the **Travel** page.

The Acadia days are intentionally movable. Each morning, put the longest exposed hike into the driest,
calmest visibility window rather than following the table rigidly.

## Highest-value choices

### Hikes

The strongest three-hike combination is **Jordan Pond + South Bubble**, **Gorham Mountain**, and
**Cadillac South Ridge**. Add Jesup Path for the arrival day or damp weather. Treat Beehive as a bonus
only when rock is dry and everyone actively wants iron rungs and exposure. The **Hikes** page has
route details, official visual links, parking backups, and shuttle advice.

### Where to stay and eat

A single downtown Bar Harbor base wins for a first four- or five-night stay: it minimizes east-side
driving, offers the broadest October dining selection, and permits walking to dinner. Start with
Yellow House Inn, Acadia Hotel–Downtown, Inn on Mount Desert, and Atlantic Oceanside, comparing
refundable total rates and parking. Reserve one special dinner—Havana is the first choice—and keep
other meals flexible. Full area tradeoffs, drive times, lodging, and restaurants are under
**Where to stay**.

### Non-hiking time

Use a clear spare day for Schoodic Peninsula or a west-side circuit through Somesville, Southwest
Harbor, Seawall, and Bass Harbor Head Light. Seal Cove Auto Museum, the Abbe Museum, La Rochelle, and
the Dorr Museum form the strongest bad-weather list. The Big Chicken Barn and giant blueberry-shaped
Wild Blueberry Land satisfy the roadside-attraction brief, though the latter is a meaningful detour.

## Book and monitor

1. Hold refundable downtown Bar Harbor lodging and a BGR round-trip rental now.
2. Price SEA–BGR, BGR–EWR, and EWR–SEA both as multi-city and separate tickets. Compare both October
   9 and 10 for the middle leg.
3. Set reminders for Cadillac Summit Road inventory: 30% releases at 10 a.m. Eastern 90 days ahead;
   70% releases at 10 a.m. Eastern two days ahead. The reservation costs $6 and is separate from the
   park pass.
4. Reserve Havana and, if desired, Jordan Pond House or a refundable boat trip; reconfirm October
   operating days shortly before travel.
5. A week before departure, check NPS conditions, National Weather Service forecasts, Maine foliage,
   NOAA tides, restaurant hours, and the reduced Island Explorer timetable.

The fare-free Island Explorer operates through **October 12, 2026**, so it covers either Acadia plan.
It is especially useful for Sand Beach/Gorham and Jordan Pond, but does not serve the Cadillac summit.
See **Check and reserve** for direct booking and monitoring links.

## Bottom line

Book the trip around **four nights in downtown Bar Harbor and a Friday October 9 flight to EWR**, while
holding Saturday as the alternate if an extra park day is more important. Build the park visit around
three movable hike days, one coast/west-side day, and ready-made rain substitutions. That structure
gets the Acadia highlights without sacrificing the family visit or gambling the trip on perfect
October weather.
